FAQ

What purchases are offered?

Fortnite offers V-Bucks and bundles that can include cosmetic items and sometimes questlines or extra rewards.

What can V-Bucks be used for?

V-Bucks can be used for cosmetic items in the Item Shop and upgrades for a season Battle Pass.

How do I add multiple gifts?

Add each item once to your cart. During checkout, the full cart is sent to the recipient you enter.

How does recipient lookup work?

Type a Fortnite username during checkout. The recipient card loads first, then unlocks the gift button after the lookup finishes.

How does checkout work?

Choose an item, recipient, and payment method, then follow the checkout steps.
